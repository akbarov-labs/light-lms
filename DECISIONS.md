2026-11-20 — Book keys are decided by code (src/lib/questions/keys.ts), not taken from the model. The pilot's book (198 pages, DTM bulletin questions 1996–2003) prints answers only inside worked solutions and in partial tables, so each question gets: the printed answer when the page shows one, the model's own solve made while reading the page (told not to copy the printed answer), and a second solve in a separate call on the transcribed text only (catches misreads as well as wrong solves). STATED = printed and both solves agree; AGREED = nothing printed and both solves agree; anything else (disagreement, a missing solve, a picture the text cannot carry) is CONFLICT/UNKNOWN and waits for a person. "Approve the sure ones" approves STATED/AGREED drafts without a picture in one press; the rest are reviewed one by one, with the printed answer, both solves and the book's solution beside each. Why two solves and not one: one model reading its own transcription repeats its own misread; a printed misprint or a misread digit should reach a person, not a student.
